# Brand Naming & Domain Strategy

## Naming Philosophy

For a customer engagement platform competing with Zoho SalesIQ, Intercom, and Drift, the name should:

- ✅ Be **short** (2-3 syllables, 5-10 characters)
- ✅ Be **memorable** and easy to spell
- ✅ **Convey** chat/engagement/intelligence
- ✅ Work **globally** (easy pronunciation)
- ✅ Have **available domains** (.com, .io, or .ai)
- ✅ Have **available social handles**

## Domain Extension Strategy

### Recommended Priority

| Extension | Best For | Price Range | Trust Level |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **.com** | Maximum credibility | $10-15/yr | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **.io** | Tech/SaaS startups | $30-50/yr | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **.ai** | AI-focused products | $70-100/yr | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **.app** | Application focus | $15-20/yr | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| **.co** | Modern alternative | $25-35/yr | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### Domain Strategy Options

**Option A: Premium .com**
```
Primary: yourname.com
Alt: yourname.io (redirect)
App: app.yourname.com
```

**Option B: Tech-Forward .io**
```
Primary: yourname.io
Alt: yourname.ai (redirect)
App: app.yourname.io
```

**Option C: AI-First .ai**
```
Primary: yourname.ai
Alt: yourname.io (redirect)
App: app.yourname.ai
```
